Our Foundation

01

Education as a
fundamental right,
not a privilege.

At Pragya Heritage International School, we hold that every learner — regardless of background, circumstance, or prior attainment — deserves access to an education of the highest calibre. Our motto, Equity is Excellence, is not a slogan. It is the institutional commitment that shapes every admission decision, every classroom practice, and every investment in student support.

Founded on the principles of the CBSE framework and inspired by the enduring values of India's heritage of learning, we bridge tradition with modernity — instilling disciplined inquiry, ethical reasoning, and intellectual confidence in each student we serve.

"When every student thrives, the institution reaches its highest potential."

— Institutional Founding Principle

Academic Programmes

A complete
CBSE learning
continuum.

From early childhood through senior secondary, our academic structure is designed with developmental integrity — each stage preparing students thoroughly for the next, while cultivating independent thought, analytical depth, and a genuine love of learning.

01

Foundational Stage

Nursery – Grade II

Play-based discovery learning grounded in NEP 2020 principles. Language, numeracy, and social skills developed through structured exploration and creative expression.

02

Preparatory Stage

Grades III – V

Consolidation of core literacies with introduction to environmental science, social studies, and structured English and Hindi proficiency across all content areas.

03

Middle School

Grades VI – VIII

Disciplinary specialisation begins. Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and Languages taught by subject experts; co-curricular integration strengthens holistic development.

04

Secondary Level

Grades IX – X

Preparation for the CBSE Class X Board examination with structured revision, internal assessment discipline, and career orientation counselling for senior years.

05

Senior Secondary

Grades XI – XII

Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams with rigorous subject preparation for CBSE Class XII Boards, JEE, NEET, and national competitive examinations.

06

Enrichment & Sports

All Grades

Performing arts, athletics, debate, and leadership programmes run parallel to academic studies — developing character, discipline, and confidence beyond the classroom.

What We Stand For

Six pillars of institutional distinction.

These commitments define how we teach, how we govern, and how we engage with every family who entrusts their child's formation to our care.

  • Academic Rigour

    Structured, research-informed pedagogy across all CBSE subjects — demanding high standards while offering the scaffolding every learner needs to meet them.

    Curriculum

  • Equitable Access

    Admission and scholarship frameworks that ensure no academically deserving student is excluded on grounds of socioeconomic circumstance — the practical expression of our motto.

    Inclusion

  • Character Formation

    Moral reasoning, civic responsibility, and respect for diverse perspectives embedded into daily school life — not as an add-on, but as core educational purpose.

    Values

  • Faculty Excellence

    Subject-specialist educators selected for both disciplinary mastery and pedagogical skill — supported through continuous professional development and institutional mentorship.

    Faculty

  • Safe & Nurturing Campus

    A secure, supervised learning environment on NH-31, Basgama — designed for physical safety, emotional wellbeing, and the dignity of every member of our school community.

    Wellbeing

  • Heritage Meets Innovation

    Reverence for India's intellectual and cultural traditions, integrated with contemporary pedagogy, digital literacy, and preparation for a rapidly evolving global landscape.

    Ethos
